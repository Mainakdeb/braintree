Braintree

An agentic framework for in-silico vision neuroscience

    Vision neuroscience is on the cusp of becoming experiment‑computable. We can present arbitrary images to in‑silico models of the human brain, measure their predicted responses, and repeat this across thousands or even millions of experimental conditions. But no framework yet makes the design of experiments scale with this newfound capacity.

    Here we introduce Braintree, an agentic framework that runs multi‑experiment studies directly on in‑silico models of human visual cortex. Given a hypothesis, it reasons over the literature, designs an experiment, builds the stimuli, runs them on brain encoding models, analyzes the responses and uses the results to design even stronger tests. It runs on its own and accepts human input at any stage, turning brain models from passive predictors of neural responses into systems that can be actively experimented on.
